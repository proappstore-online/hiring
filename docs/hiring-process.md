## New LinkedIn applicants
- Check runs on Serge's RL laptop (Sergeys-RL-Laptop.local) in Chrome, read-only, using the one specific Chrome account signed in there — Profile 3, sivochkin@gmail.com. Not "whatever account" — one known Chrome account.

- The FreeGameStore Developer Intern post is paused at its free limit of ten applicants.

## Acknowledgement email
- Sent from admin@freeappstore.online, subject: "Your application to Developer Intern - Unpaid Volunteer at FreeGameStore".
- Body: thank them for applying via LinkedIn, say the team is reviewing and will be in touch, then state the criteria — daily 9 AM Melbourne-time meetings, attend at least two each week; at least 8 hours per week; 10-week internship with certificate or referral on completion — and ask them to reply confirming they can meet the criteria.
- Hasitha-style variant: if the applicant is outside Australia/NZ, add the location transparency paragraph (copied from Saba's Saturday email) before the criteria.
- Nothing is sent until Serge reviews the drafts.
