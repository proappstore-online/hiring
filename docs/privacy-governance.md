# Privacy, retention and unpaid-arrangement governance

This is an engineering policy proposal. The owner and qualified legal reviewer must approve jurisdiction-specific notices, periods and unpaid classification before public launch. Starter legal pages are placeholders.

## Processing and consent

Collect only requisition-relevant fields. Separate mandatory processing notice from optional talent-pool consent and optional communications. Record notice/version, purpose, timestamp, source and affirmative action; optional consent is never prechecked. Candidate links are short-lived and single-purpose. Restrict reviewer access by assignment. Do not collect protected traits for selection unless separately required and governed. Candidate data may not train an AI model or go to an external model without a separately approved basis and controls.

## Retention

Configure retention by organization, jurisdiction and classification before opening intake. Maintain distinct clocks for incomplete submissions, rejected/withdrawn applications, hired applications, attachments, message bodies, audit evidence and optional pools. **Decision pending:** exact periods and lawful bases need legal review. Until approved, do not publish an application form. Expiration jobs delete storage and indexes and record content-free completion evidence. A legal hold needs reason, owner, scope, review/expiry date and audit; it suspends only affected deletion. Backups expire on a documented schedule and restores replay deletion tombstones.

## Subject rights and incidents

Support access/export, correction, withdrawal and erasure requests. Verify identity proportionately without disclosing another brand's application. Track deadline, owner, receipt, decision and completion by jurisdiction. Export the subject's data safely, excluding confidential reviewer material where required. Propagate erasure/rectification to MAIL and installed processors under documented contracts. Audit exports and rights access. Maintain incident triage, containment, affected-scope, notification decision and evidence; legal owner sets notification timelines.

## Unpaid-arrangement gate

Every requisition declares paid employment, paid contract, volunteer, internship, work trial or other, with jurisdiction, duties, duration, supervision, benefit, expenses and compensation. Candidate copy must reflect the true terms. Volunteer/internship/trial/other unpaid types enter `pending_review`; publication and offer dispatch are blocked until a named approver records jurisdiction-specific legal review, rationale, evidence, conditions and expiry. Changed terms invalidate approval. Calling work a trial or internship does not establish lawful nonpayment. Uncertain cases escalate to owner/legal reviewer; approval is never automated. Audit approval and candidate-facing disclosure version.

Before real intake, approve owner/contact identity, legal pages, notice and periods; test rights/deletion, cross-brand denial and unpaid blocks; confirm MAIL processor terms; complete threat model and incident runbook. Release owner signs the evidence record.
