# Privacy, retention and unpaid-arrangement governance

This records the owner-approved policy for [issue #9](https://github.com/proappstore-online/hiring/issues/9), including [Serge's decisions in issue #52 on 2026-10-21](https://github.com/proappstore-online/hiring/issues/52#issuecomment-6309937556). It defines policy rather than claiming every rights-handling feature is already implemented.

## Legal basis for processing

Process account details and user-created content for contract performance: delivering the service and features the user signed up for and requested. Legitimate interest covers providing, securing and improving the service. Cookieless, non-identifying aggregate analytics uses legitimate interest in understanding aggregate usage, as stated in the public Privacy Policy; optional communications and talent-pool participation require separate consent. No special-category personal data is collected; forms must not request it. Payment records, where applicable, are retained to meet legal obligations.

## Processing and consent

Collect only requisition-relevant fields. Separate mandatory processing notice from optional talent-pool consent and optional communications. Record notice/version, purpose, timestamp, source and affirmative action; optional consent is never prechecked. Candidate links are short-lived and single-purpose. Restrict reviewer access by assignment. Candidate data may not train an AI model or go to an external model without a separately approved basis and controls.

## Retention matrix

| Data category | Retention period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account details (ID, name, avatar, email from OAuth provider) | Indefinitely while account exists; deleted on account-deletion request | Contract performance |
| User-created content | Indefinitely while account exists; deleted on account-deletion request | Contract performance |
| Usage/analytics data (cookieless, non-identifying) | Indefinitely (aggregated, non-personal) | Legitimate interest |
| Payment records (where applicable) | As required by applicable law | Legal obligation |

Serge approved indefinite retention on 2026-10-21 in issue #52, answering the retention decision in issue #9. There is no automatic purge or automated deletion schedule. Account details and user-created content remain stored until an explicit account-deletion request, except records that applicable law requires us to keep. Stopping use, rejection or withdrawal does not trigger automatic deletion. This policy includes application content, attachments and message bodies; it does not create an expiry clock for those records. Non-identifying aggregate analytics is retained indefinitely.

## Subject rights and incidents

Support access/export, correction, withdrawal and erasure requests. Verify identity proportionately without disclosing another brand's application. Track deadline, owner, receipt, decision and completion by jurisdiction. Export the subject's data safely, excluding confidential reviewer material where required. Propagate erasure/rectification to MAIL and installed processors under documented contracts. Audit exports and rights access. Maintain incident triage, containment, affected-scope, notification decision and evidence; the operator owns applicable notification timelines. Indefinite retention does not remove subject rights or legally required deletion duties.

## Owner approval and legal pages

Serge approved no external legal review gate on 2026-10-21 in issue #52. The legal pages reflect the owner-approved decisions and do not require lawyer sign-off before publishing. This is an owner product decision, not a finding of legal compliance. The owner maintains the Privacy Policy, Terms, contact identity and processor arrangements; this document does not invent a controller identity, jurisdiction or statutory deadline where none has been supplied.

## Unpaid arrangements

Every requisition declares paid employment, paid contract, volunteer, internship, work trial or other, with jurisdiction, duties, duration, supervision, benefit, expenses and compensation. Candidate copy must reflect the true terms. Unpaid, internship and volunteer roles have no legal-review approval gate at this stage, consistent with issue #52 and the closure of issue #10. Calling work a trial or internship does not establish lawful nonpayment. Record the candidate-facing disclosure version and changes to the terms.

Before real intake, verify owner/contact identity, published notices and processor arrangements; test rights/deletion and cross-brand denial; complete the threat model and incident runbook. The release owner records the evidence. No external legal review or automatic retention expiry is a release prerequisite.
